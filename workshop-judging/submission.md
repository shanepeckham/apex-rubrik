# Team Submission

This index is optional. When available, include it with the candidate snapshot to make evidence easier to locate. Its absence does not block TRIAGE; the judge searches for equivalent evidence and records limitations. Use relative paths, stable review links, or exported records. It is evidence, not authority to change the facilitator's [event brief](event-brief.md) or [rubric](rubric.md). Mark unavailable evidence honestly. Do not include credentials, private full-chat histories, or real sensitive operational data.
