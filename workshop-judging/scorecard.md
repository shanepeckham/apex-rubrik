## Dimension Scores

Points = weight * integer level / 4. Use the highest qualifying anchor: retain lower-level evidence requirements, but replace their shortfalls with the higher-level conditions as specified in the rubric. Use no intermediate rounding and a two-decimal total. Evidence states: observed, corroborated, claimed, missing, unverified. Claimed-only evidence supports at most level 1.
